## What the product must do

1. **Stop the leaks forever:** The autonomous `never-stop` loop must be hard-wired through the same file-placement + blueprint-authority gates that `execute-batch` uses.
2. **Make the strict gate green:** `lifeos:bp-priority:verify` (migration preflight, canonical execution spine, typed blockers, dual-path spine, product-home verify) must pass.
3. **Pay down SSOT debt:** Every source file under the protected prefixes must either have a valid `@ssot` or an explicit grandfathering decision with a recorded baseline.
4. **Close false-done drift:** SOFT content-drift findings must be remediated or waived with receipts.
5. **Build the Receipt Auditor / Reality Replay Agent:** Any `PASS` or `DONE` receipt can be re-run at its pinned git SHA and proven to produce the same result.
6. **Make SENTRY real:** SENTRY must run real-browser/human-sim walkthroughs and produce Layer A + Layer B receipts with separation of duties.
7. **Wire the Chair/Lens stack into the build pipeline:** The Chair must compose responsibilities + lenses and select the cheapest capable model before executing.
8. **Measure model ROI:** Every model call must be logged with cost, outcome, and lens trust-score feedback.
9. **Prove revenue reality:** SMOS (or another product) must process a real paid transaction and produce a SENTRY receipt.
10. **Self-heal:** Wisdom must update lens trust scores and product BUILD_QUEUEs from reality data.
11. **Demonstrate the full loop:** One product must go from founder idea → approved blueprint → code → deployed reality → measured revenue → learned improvement.

## Cognitive architecture disambiguation

- **BuilderOS reasoning stack:** Mission → Responsibility → Lens/Cognitive Asset → Model → Execution. Models are interchangeable engines. Lenses are reusable, versioned, evidence-backed Cognitive Assets that the Chair composes.
- **Cognitive Core (docs/constitution/COGNITIVE_CORE_LAWS.md):** A separate product that models a person (Adam or a client) across a seven-layer stack (Identity, Programs, Situation, Perspective, Attention, Reasoning, Calibration). It is **not** the BuilderOS Chair and must not be converged into it.
- **Reusable math only:** BuilderOS reuses the generic, zero-coupled calibration functions in `services/cognitive-core-oracle.js` (Brier scoring, `decideGate`, `verdictFromReceipt`) for lens/model trust. The user-specific judgment tables (`judgment_decisions` / `judgment_outcomes`) stay inside the Cognitive Core product.

## Who it serves

- BuilderOS itself — the machine that builds everything else.

## What it should feel like

The system receives a founder idea and does not immediately write code. It first produces a digital twin (`FOUNDER_PACKET` + `BLUEPRINT` + `DECISION_RECORDS`), activates the right responsibilities and lenses, preserves disagreement before synthesis, selects the cheapest capable model, manufactures each step, and then a separate, independent agent verifies every claim against the running deployment and real user behavior. If reality does not match the twin, the system rolls back only the failed step, learns why, and tries again.

## Important constraints

- No human bottleneck in routine manufacture.
- AI may propose, analyze, challenge, and synthesize, but it may not silently invent founder intent or approve unresolved product decisions.
- No `PASS` or `DONE` without an independent verifier or an explicit `separation_collapsed` reason.
- No new `services/` or `routes/` files without `@ssot` and a product home.
- No paid model spending without token budget and ROI measurement.
- The `never-stop` loop does not resume until all hard gates are fail-closed.
- Routine phase transitions are self-authorized; real-money steps and final 10/10 ratification require founder sign-off.
