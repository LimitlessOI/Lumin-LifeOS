## 1. The One Engine

There is not a Website Engine, a Video Engine, a Marketing Engine, and a Life Engine. There is **one engine** with different **renderers** on top (website, video, email, ad, social post, sales script, coaching conversation, AI-receptionist call).

The engine answers one question:

> **Given what we know about this person right now, what is the next *voluntary* step that creates the greatest long-term value for them?**

- **External name (customer-facing):** *Creative Engine* — simple, unremarkable, not exposed publicly. **Locked, not open to debate.**
- **Internal name:** does not matter and is not public. The functions inside it: mission contract, journey positioning, earned-attention budget, objection modeling, competitive-gap analysis, confidence scoring, decision simulation, outcome learning.

The customer buys creative output. Internally, the system produces it by optimizing the next **truthful, voluntary** step — not by generating content.

## 2. The Laws (candidate)

Each law is tagged with the **Law-Category Test** (see §4) so we never inflate a strategy into a law.

**Law 1 — Reality is the scoreboard.** *(empirical)* Not confidence, not opinion, not intuition. Every prediction, taste-score, forecast, or persona-critique is a **hypothesis** until reality measures it. Founder wording (preferred, because lived): *"Results — often harsh, always fair. Measure them. If you don't like the results, make different choices."* Short handle: "reality is the scoreboard."

**Law 2 — People own their goals.** *(governance)* The system never decides what someone *should* want; it helps them get what *they* decided matters. If they change their mind, that is success, not failure.

**Law 3 — Always earn the next step.** *(governance/strategy)* Never ask for trust, attention, commitment, or a sale you have not earned. Content is a chain of micro-contracts (earn 3s → 10s → 30s → …). Optimize for the next *willing* step, not the final outcome.

**Law 4 — Influence without coercion.** *(governance; falsifiable proxy)* Never remove a person's ability to freely choose. **Falsifiable proxy (the actual gate):** did the system re-pursue the original ask after an explicit opt-out? **Format-level rule:** no growth incentive (referral pressure, upsell, streak-shame) may override an opt-out. *The real test of "no manipulation" is what happens when someone says no.*

**Law 5 — Build capability, not dependency.** *(governance)* If someone needs the system forever, the system failed. Purpose is increasing agency until they need it less. **OPEN:** the business-model tension (subscription incentives reward retention) is unresolved — lead candidate is Willhite's "paid in proportion to service rendered": a graduated user refers/expands across the ecosystem rather than churns.

**Law 6 — Progress happens in relationship. *(proposed addition)*** *(governance)* The system supports the person's connection to **real people** and never substitutes itself for it. Rationale: Self-Determination Theory's third validated need (relatedness) is otherwise unaddressed; and a system that becomes a person's *only* relationship is building dependency — so Law 6 also **guards Law 5**.

**Companion principle (pairs with Law 1):** *Reality tells us **whether** we succeeded; human dignity constrains **how** we are allowed to succeed.* A manipulative message that gets more clicks is not adopted just because reality says it "worked." Law 1 and Laws 2/4 are coupled on purpose.

## 3. Enforcement architecture — hard-gate the reasoning, not the behavior

Nothing renders until a structured intake exists: **Mission · Customer · current/desired decision stage · primary objection · evidence required · trust strategy · next voluntary action · success metric.** The exact fields are not sacred; forcing the questions to be answered is.

- **Already a proven pattern here:** BPB requires a spec before Architect/Builder acts; SENTRY requires assertions before a step is "done." Same shape, new domain.
- **Necessary but not sufficient — the coherence layer:** a presence-check can be gamed by a placeholder (the "13 bugs" lesson: an exports-smoke check passed garbage because it only checked a name existed). The gate needs a second, **adversarial coherence check** — does the Objection follow from the Customer stage; does the Trust Strategy address the stated Objection — the same "verify-the-factory" principle as the SENTRY chaos-canary, aimed at content.
- **Decision Brief / simulate-before-commit** (= Stoic *premeditatio malorum*): for **significant** goals, simulate costs/benefits/risks/uncertainties before commit, then compare to reality after N months (calibration loop). **Stakes threshold required** (Gate Charter: block only irreversible/high-blast-radius) or it becomes analysis-paralysis and violates Law 3. Career/financial/major-health = full brief; a daily micro-step = no brief, just forward motion.
- **Decision Simulator** (persona critique before publish) is **Hypothesis-grade** until its scores are calibrated against real outcomes (Law 1 applied to itself) — route through the existing calibration-sampling mechanism or it is theater in a UX costume.

## 4. The Law-Category Test (governance tool)

Before anything is called a **Law**, classify it:

1. **Empirical** — a testable claim about reality (e.g. Law 1). Can be a Law.
2. **Governance** — a procedurally-verifiable rule (e.g. "the AI never overrode the stated goal" — checkable yes/no). Can be a Law.
3. **Default strategy** — itself *subject to Law 1* (e.g. Be→Do→Have, coaching style, media routing). **Never a Law** — it is the current best-evidence default, replaced when reality shows better for a specific person.

This prevents philosophy-inflation and keeps the doctrine falsifiable rather than dogmatic.

## 5. One substrate, two graphs

The same **predict → measure → calibrate** primitive already shipped (Cognitive Core Outcome Oracle + self-repair Tier-0 stores) underlies code repair, marketing decisions, *and* life-coaching. Build **one substrate, two instantiations** — not parallel systems:

- **Constitutional Knowledge Graph** (company level): principles as nodes, domains as edges; queried by "which principles apply to this new problem," not by chat history. Partial precursors already exist (`self-repair-root-cause-chains.js`, the Unified Doctrine Map) — both flat tables today, not yet a graph.
- **Life Graph** (per user): Identity as the center node; Health/Business/Relationships/Purpose/Learning as edges that ripple into each other. Cold-start from best general human-development evidence, personalize from real interaction (same cold-start pattern as a fresh codegen target's reputation score).

**Guardrail (protects Law 2):** if the system models "the kind of person who builds wealth" it must offer those as **hypotheses for the person to evaluate**, never prescribe them — otherwise the system chooses the goal by the back door.

## 8. Creative Media Stack, Observatory & Human-at-Center Provenance (2026-07-21)

Extends the One Engine (§1) to the media renderers (video, image, voice, likeness/lip-sync, website). Same candidate status as the rest of this doc. Load-bearing modules build through the governed factory (SO-001); all scheduled grading/scan calls run under the Zero-Waste AI Call guard.

**8.1 Capability library ≠ menu.** Wire every best-in-class tool we might want; *"present"* and *"offered"* are separate switches (entitlement-flagged). Image / likeness / voice-clone ships **founder-only** until real consent capture + NO-FAKES-aligned likeness controls exist. Customers use the system themselves before any automation-for-them unlock. We are never forced to serve what we've plumbed.

**8.2 The Engine Observatory (extend, don't just consume).** Every external engine is a *subject under observation*, not just a vendor. The Creative Model Registry keeps a living **dossier** per tool: what it's best at, *why* (techniques we can glean), web-intel on their methods, its measured performance frontier over time, and its **disclosure footprint** (does it stamp C2PA/SynthID; does it falsely tag edits as generative). Extends `chair-competitive-research.js` from *product* competitors → to *model providers*. [KNOW gap: no media market-scan exists today.]

**8.3 Grading + Market Scanner — the founder decision (Chair-ratified strategy).** Router chooses per capability by quality-floor × speed × cost. Grading is **Balanced**: weekly synthetic benchmark across active models + continuous real-render logging + immediate grade on any new model the scanner finds — with a **market-pace auto-escalation valve** (scanner tracks new-model arrival rate; if it exceeds the weekly cadence, grading frequency auto-bumps, no re-decision). Spend is **lean-and-earn**: ride Replicate/ElevenLabs we already pay for, add premium only when grading or a paying job justifies it, with **one cheap lip-sync provider onboarded now** to close the real gap; money cap stays founder-ratified. Decided with the live Chair (Cognitive Core, strong-model tier; capsules founder/customer/anti_you; `decision_id ff0ea2bb-fabb-454a-bf26-113e7e2685f0`, `prediction_id 91620f23-20e2-43d7-8af7-e5a913045c1a`; option_b @0.85).

**8.4 Block — Replacement-Readiness Oracle (velocity-gated build-vs-buy).** Per capability, track three curves: our quality vs theirs on the same benchmark, cost delta, and improvement *slopes*. Recommend "build our own engine" **only** when our frontier crosses theirs AND our slope is steeper (we'll out-improve them, so we won't fall behind again). Implements Adam's rule: replace them "only when we can outperform them *faster than they can*." Runs on the Cognitive Core predict→measure→calibrate scoreboard.

**8.5 Block — "They train us" capture.** Every production call logs the input→output pair (brief/prompt/assets → render + quality grade) into a proprietary, capability-tagged distillation corpus. Their API spend simultaneously funds the training set to replace them; combined with dossier technique notes, our future engine gets a warm start the day the Oracle greenlights it. Cheap to start (logging on renders we already run); compounds silently.

**8.6 Block — Marginal-value router + premium pass-through.** Routing signal is **Δquality ÷ Δcost**, not raw quality. A slightly-better, much-pricier model wins only when the edge is decision-relevant: a high-stakes asset (hero / thumbnail / brand face — flagged + justified), the customer bought up, or an A/B-proven edge. Default tier = value model, bundled, no surcharge. **Premium render = opt-in, cost-plus tack-on** (covers provider delta + small margin); never a silent auto-burn. "Is the slight edge worth it?" is answered **empirically (Law 1)** via A/B on real engagement, not a hard-coded hunch. Every premium-vs-value choice a customer makes feeds the observatory (what people pay more for).

**8.7 Human-at-Center Provenance Standard (the moat).** North star (Adam): become the standard for how ethical, human-centered AI-assisted content is done — *whether or not YouTube ever notices.* "Humans at the center" is worthless unless verifiable, so it must be **provable, not a slogan** (Law 1 applied to ethics).
- **Disclosure-class tag on every render** (grounded in 2026 YouTube policy, KNOW): `exempt_edit` (footage_edit, photo_polish, own-voice repair, color/trim/sharpen/upscale — YouTube-exempt) · `requires_disclosure` (lip-sync fabricating *new* words in a real mouth, *other-person* voice/likeness, generative scene of a real place/event) · `auto_labeled` (C2PA-fully-generative or SynthID-watermarked → *permanent* YouTube label). **Refined line (Adam):** self-correction/repair = editing (fixing your own stumble in your own voice is not synthesis); fabricating a whole statement never made = synthetic. It's **degree + intent-to-mislead**, not "lip-sync yes/no."
- **Provenance receipt per render**, auto-derived from the job telemetry log (original capture → each action → output, tool + timestamp) = an instant, tamper-evident dispute pack against a *false* auto-label, and a user-facing, shareable "made with humans at the center" credential (the free marketing surface).
- **Truthful, explicit C2PA Content Credentials** (spec 2.4): ordered `c2pa.actions` (v2) with per-action `digitalSourceType` (`digitalCapture` / `algorithmicallyEnhanced` vs `trainedAlgorithmicMedia` / `compositedWithTrainedAlgorithmicMedia`), `softwareAgent`, and `ingredientIds` to the source. The spec **forbids redacting the actions history** → a permanent honest record. Requires a **trust-listed signing certificate** (not a self-sign) or platforms treat it as unverified. Honest caveats: manifests strip on re-encode; SynthID (Google, not ours) is the harder generative signal; explicitness *correctly* surfaces genuine synthesis — a truth amplifier, not a loophole.
- **Monetization reality (KNOW, 2026):** a disclosure label does **not** reduce reach/monetization; the real risk is YouTube's *"inauthentic content"* policy (mass-produced / templated / generic AI slop → demonetized at channel level). This **validates** the SMOS premise (real human story → original content) and turns our "no template-echo, real-AI-or-fail-closed" doctrine into a **compliance feature** for our users.
- **The standard selects the customer:** radical transparency magnetizes authenticity-selling creators/brands and is irrelevant to content-farms (not our ICP). Publish the standard openly + adhere verifiably; **adoption follows proof, not permission** (how C2PA itself became a standard).

**8.8 Build anchoring (extend, not greenfield) & open items.** Live audit (2026-07-21) confirms: image gen (Replicate: Flux 1.1 Pro / Ideogram v3 / Recraft v3-SVG) and voice/TTS (ElevenLabs + OpenAI fallback) are **LIVE**; script→video **PARTIAL**; **lip-sync/likeness, the media router, model grading/scoreboard, negative-render KB, and market scanner are the real gaps** (GRAIL spec-only). Extend `services/creative-engine/index.js` + `services/video-pipeline.js`; do **not** build on `core/video-generator.js`, `creatorTextToVideo.js`, or the in-memory likeness route. **First build brick** = a media telemetry table + writing `cost_actual_cents` / latency / `generation_mode` / `ai_disclosure_class` / provenance per render — it is *simultaneously* the silent-failure fix, the grading substrate, and the provenance-receipt source. Open: which single lip-sync provider (Hedra vs D-ID) + factory build sequencing — to be worked through the Chair, not asked of Adam.
