# Both sides — ways to make money on the way up AND down

**Command:** `npm run lip:blind:both`  
**Receipt:** `data/lip/blind-sim-both-sides.json`

## Simple map

| Direction | Success trail | Failure trail (don’t do this) |
|-----------|---------------|-------------------------------|
| **Way UP** | Volume explodes + price drifts up **before** the public shout → buy small → **sell when shout hits** | Buy the public shout / chase after it’s already up |
| **Way DOWN** | After the public shout → **short the first down minute** | Short while it’s still mooning; buy the dump thinking it’s a dip |

## What the blind tests showed ($10k paper)

**Down path pays.** Shorts made the money on every segment (example: segment F shorts ≈ **+$720**).

**Public “guess the pump from tape alone” longs lose.** On every segment, those longs were **red in dollars** and dragged the combined book below short-only.

**Early info (know the shout is coming soon) can help the up path** — sometimes (B up ≈ +$119). Not reliable every segment.

**Fresh unused G (2019 Aug–early Sep, ~5 weeks of pump events):**
- Short only: **+2.4%**
- Early-info both: **+2.2%**
- Public both: **+1.5%** (longs hurt)
- Buy the shout: **−8.8%**

## Working formula (not one trade — a stack)

1. **Core down sleeve (works now with public shout feed):** short first drop. Small size. That’s the proven edge.
2. **Up sleeve only with early eyes** (VIP / schedule / private channel timing): buy the wake ~5–12 minutes early → sell into the public shout. Skip if you only see the public call.
3. **Never** buy the public shout on the way up.
4. Bigger capital (BTC hold) is separate — this sleeve is event trading.

## Time scale (plain)

These percents are from **clusters of pump events over days/weeks**, not “+X% every 6 months.”
