## 2. Common “about to take off” signs (buy-side checklist)

These show up again and again in blogs (Changelly, Bitsgap, CoinXSight/OBV guides, Glassnode OI, funding playbooks) and in 2024–25 case writeups:

### A. Quiet buying before the pop (subtle)

1. **Price sideways / tight range** (triangle, rectangle, flag pause)  
2. **Volume / OBV rising while price is flat** → accumulation (“volume precedes price”)  
3. **Higher lows** into a flat top (pressure building)  
4. **Bollinger squeeze** (bands tight) then expansion  

### B. Confirmation when it breaks (less subtle)

5. **Daily/4H close above clear resistance** (not a wick fake)  
6. **Volume ≥ ~1.5–2× recent average** on the break candle  
7. **RSI reclaiming >50** (momentum flipped up)  
8. **MACD bullish cross / histogram expanding** after quiet  

### C. Derivatives (modern crypto — very important 2024–26)

9. **Open interest rising with price** (new money, not just short cover)  
10. **Funding mild positive** (~0.02–0.06% / 8h) — interest without mania  
11. **Avoid** extreme funding + extreme OI + Extreme Greed (often near tops / liquidations)

### D. Story + attention (blogs & comms)

12. **Clear narrative** people can repeat (ETF, halving, AI, L2, privacy)  
13. **Search / social volume rising** — but **peak Google/Twitter often marks the event or local top**, not always the best early entry  
14. **2024 BTC lesson:** ETF approval spiked searches **as** the news hit; much of the big run was **inflows + structure** over weeks, with retail search still below 2021 mania — i.e. **money flow ≠ retail buzz**

### Easy “must see most of these” rule (THINK)

```
RANGE + rising OBV/volume
AND close above resistance on high volume
AND RSI > 50
AND (OI up with price OR spot volume strong)
AND funding not insane
AND BTC not in freefall
→ candidate takeoff long (paper first)
```

False breakout = break without volume, or OI/funding blow-off already at Extreme Greed.

## 3. Blogs / communications — do they confirm?

**Yes — same trail, repeated:**

| Source type | What they say before runs |
|-------------|---------------------------|
| Breakout blogs (Changelly, Bitsgap, etc.) | Structure → volume → RSI/MACD confirm |
| OBV guides | OBV breaks / rises before price often |
| Derivatives blogs (OI, funding, Glassnode) | Price+OI up = real; crowded funding = danger |
| Sentiment guides (Fear&Greed, social) | Fear can be opportunity; Extreme Greed late |
| 2024 ETF case studies | Narrative + volume/inflows; search peaks with news; don’t buy only the headline spike |

**Comms lag warning (same as our P&D study):** when something is **everywhere** on Reddit/X/YouTube, you’re often late for the *start*. Early trail = quieter accumulation + rising OI/volume + story forming, not peak memes.
