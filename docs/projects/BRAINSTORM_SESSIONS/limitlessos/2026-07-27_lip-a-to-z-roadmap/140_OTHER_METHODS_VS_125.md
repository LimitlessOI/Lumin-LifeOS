# Other investment options vs +125%/month

**Goal:** +125% in a month (not called impossible — not solved yet).  
**Question:** Are there methods with a **better chance** of that kind of result than our current proved stack?

**Short answer:** **Yes — but only the high-variance ones.** Steady methods (funding, options income, BTC hold) almost never print +125% in a month. Methods that *can* need **asymmetric upside + speed + (often) leverage** — and they can also go to zero. We pursue them in a **capped “stretch sleeve,”** not with the whole book.

---

## Honest split

| Bucket | Can hit +125%/mo? | Typical reality | Fit for LIP |
|--------|-------------------|-----------------|-------------|
| **A. Steady / structural** | Almost never | +0.5–3%/mo when working | Core / floor |
| **B. Market beta** | Rare (only extreme bulls) | Great years, ugly bears | Core growth |
| **C. Stretch / fat-tail** | **Yes, some months** | Most months flat/red; some months huge | Capped lottery + research |

**+125%/month lives in bucket C.** Bucket A/B fund survival and compounding while C hunts the mile.

---

## Methods ranked for “chance of that kind of month”

### Better chance of a stretch month (bucket C)

| Method | Why it can print huge | Failure mode | Non-reckless rule |
|--------|----------------------|--------------|-------------------|
| **1. Long options / LEAPS on high-beta** (stocks or crypto-related) | Defined risk (premium); multi-bagger if underlying rips | Most options expire worthless | Risk only **1–3% of book** per idea |
| **2. Concentrated leveraged crypto** (2–5×, not 50×) on confirmed breakout/news | Volatility + leverage = month can 2×+ | Liquidation / −80% sleeves | Hard daily stop; tiny % of total capital |
| **3. New listing / catalyst snipes** (IPO, TGE, ETF flow days) | Gaps and flows create outsized days | Late entry = bagholder | Rules-based; size cap; trail |
| **4. Early narrative / venture-like** (seed, private, or “know before crowd”) | One winner pays for many losers | Illiquid; most die; not monthly mark | Separate illiquid bucket; don’t need monthly liquidity |
| **5. Prop-style discretionary** with proven edge + leverage | Skilled traders sometimes have monster months | Most retail blow up | Only after paper edge; firm-like caps |
| **6. Limitless / P&D fade + early-up** (what we study) | Fat tails on events | Public-late longs lose | Keep short; early only with real info |

### Worse chance of +125%/mo (but better for not dying)

| Method | Why |
|--------|-----|
| Funding harvest / basis | Often high-single to mid-teens **per year**, not per month |
| Options **income** (condors, covered calls) | Built for ~**1–3%/month**, not 125% |
| Forex carry / momentum | Solid research edges; not moonshot months |
| BTC/ETH hold | Can have great **years**; rarely +125% in one **month** outside mania |
| Our measured P&D short sleeve alone | Green clusters of **~1–7%** over weeks — path to 125%/mo needs scale/leverage/more events |

---

## What actually improves odds vs “just more P&D longs”

For the **stretch goal**, research says raise odds by combining:

1. **Asymmetry** — risk $1 to make $5–20 (options, small venture stakes), not risk $1 to make $1.  
2. **Leverage only on confirmed setups** — and only inside a loss cap.  
3. **More independent shots** — fleet of small accounts / ideas (charter already says this).  
4. **Early information** — filings, calendars, on-chain, research — not late FOMO.  
5. **Barbell** — most capital in B (BTC/steady); small % in C (stretch). Taleb-style: don’t die, still catch fat tails.

---

## Recommended LIP shape toward the goal

```
80–90%  Core: BTC/ETH + funding/steady (survive)
 5–15%  Active: breakout+trail, P&D first-drop short
 0–5%   Stretch: options / leveraged breakout / catalyst snipes
         → only this sleeve is allowed to chase +125%/mo math
```

If the stretch sleeve hits, it can move the **whole book** a lot.  
If it dies, the core still stands. That’s non-reckless pursuit of a hard goal.

## Labels

- **KNOW:** Income options and funding are not built for +125%/month. Concentrated leverage and long options *can* produce that magnitude in a month — and wipe the sleeve.  
- **THINK:** Best *chance* at the goal = capped asymmetric stretch sleeve + many trials, not resizing the whole book.  
- **DON’T KNOW:** Which stretch variant wins for *us* until we paper them side by side.
