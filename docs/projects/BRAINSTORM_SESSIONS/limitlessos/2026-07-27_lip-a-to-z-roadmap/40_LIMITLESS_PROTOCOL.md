## What Limitless Protocol *is*

We do **not** create pump-and-dumps. We **identify** public hype / P&D / trend patterns and may trade them — small accounts, scale-out, get out.

See the **sea of patterns** before the crowd fully prices them → classify (legit vs hype/P&D) → invest in **smart, small, many** ways → **get out when needed**.

**Sibling priority:** For **lowest risk / solid return chance**, prefer funding harvest + BTC hold + regime DCA (`30_CRYPTO_FOREX_RESEARCH.md`) — Limitless is the higher-variance narrative sleeve.

### Are 15–20/month even real? (research)

| Evidence | Rate | Label |
|----------|------|-------|
| Finance Research Letters sample (1,457 pumps, Jan 2018–Jan 2022) | **~11 / month** average (~3/week) | KNOW — coordinated Telegram-style in that study |
| Imperial/Xu-era Telegram channel scrape (~8 months, 412 events) | Roughly **~50 / month** in that hot window; channels coordinated **~2/day** aggregate | KNOW for that period — not every year |
| Broader literature | Hundreds–thousands over multi-year spans; activity **clusters** and shifts exchanges | KNOW |
| **Our catchable set** | Subset of raw events (speed, liquidity, false positives) | THINK: **8–20 identifiable/month** is plausible in active regimes; **quiet months may be &lt;10** |

**Verdict:** 15–20/month is an **aspirational identify target**, not a guarantee. Some months the ocean has that many coordinated pumps; some months it doesn’t. We measure actual `# identified / month` on paper — if the sea is thin, we don’t invent events.

Devil’s advocate: “Identified” ≠ “profitable entry.” Organizers still eat first; we need early tape + strict exits.

## Human-understanding → investing (group level)

LifeOS / Lumin already care about humans deeply (patterns, fears, recognition). For **LIP**, that only helps when applied to **groups / crowds**, never stalking one private person for trades.

| Human layer | Invest use |
|-------------|------------|
| **Fear / greed cycles** | Risk-on vs risk-off; when to size down; dump-phase tells |
| **Recognition / status seeking** | Hype coins, meme stocks, “being early” FOMO — Limitless Protocol fuel |
| **Herding** | Bipartisan Congress clusters, social mention velocity, copycat flows |
| **Narrative belief** | Legit story vs empty pump — classifier input |
| **Movement of attention** | Where eyes go before where price goes (sometimes) |

**Verdict (THINK):** Yes — deep group-level human understanding is a **real edge input** for LIP, especially Limitless Protocol (hype vs legit) and disclosure/sentiment sleeves. It does **not** replace tape, costs, or exits. Individuals stay out of the gun sight (ethics + noise).

**Fence:** Aggregate / public discourse only. No weaponizing private therapy-grade data on a named person to front-run them.

## Exit design — capture + trail (Adam 2026-07-27)

Split the fleet on every event so some **lock upside** and some **trail up**:

| Tranche | ~Share of accounts | Exit |
|---------|-------------------|------|
| **Secure** | ~1/3 | Early fixed target (~35% of typical swing) — bank seed + gain |
| **Core** | ~1/3 | Fixed ~60–70% of typical swing — main capture |
| **Runner** | ~1/3 | **Trailing stop**: arm after ~40% of swing; trail ~12% off peak — ride further, still cut on dump |

Hard floor stop + short time-stop on all. Runners never “hope forever.”

That’s how we get out enough to be safe **and** let some ride the trail up.
