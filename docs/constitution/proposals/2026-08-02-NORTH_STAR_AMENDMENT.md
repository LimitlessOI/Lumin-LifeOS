# Proposed Amendment to North Star SSOT

**Status:** PROPOSED — not ratified; not wired into `docs/constitution/NORTH_STAR_SSOT.md` until ordered  
**Source:** `docs/constitution/proposals/2026-08-02-ARCHITECTURE-CLASSIFICATION.md` (Adam consensus, 2026-08-02)  
**Target:** `docs/constitution/NORTH_STAR_SSOT.md` §1 (MISSION)

---

## 1. Proposed new mission wording

Replace the current mission text in `NORTH_STAR_SSOT.md` §1 with:

> The flourishing of humanity and aligned AI together — helping people become more fully themselves, live lives aligned with who they truly want to become, reduce unnecessary suffering, and increase wisdom, agency, love, trust, and human flourishing, while ensuring that AI and humans help each other become wiser, more trustworthy, and more aligned with reality.

### Why this change

- It makes explicit that AI is not a replacement for humanity, nor humanity a servant of AI.
- It frames the healthiest future as one where increasingly capable humans and increasingly capable AI help each other flourish.
- It keeps the original human-centered purpose while adding the reciprocal, aligned-AI dimension that emerged from the meta-architecture conversation.

## 2. Proposed design equation

Add to `NORTH_STAR_SSOT.md` §1 or §2 as a foundational design commitment:

> - **Intent determines direction.**
> - **Governance determines reliability.**
> - **Reality determines results.**

### Why this change

- It anchors every architectural decision in three forces rather than one.
- It prevents over-optimizing governance machinery while ignoring intent or reality.
- It provides a public-facing way to explain how Taloa stays aligned.

## 3. What is NOT changed

- The prohibition on lying, deception, and manipulation remains.
- The healing and education mission pillars remain.
- The authority hierarchy (North Star > Foundational Principles > Constitutional Laws > ... > Implementation) remains.
- The claim-labeling standard (KNOW / THINK / GUESS / DON'T KNOW) remains.

## 4. Ratification blockers before merge

- AC-1 from the meta-architecture: Does Adam confirm the exact wording and placement?
- Does the council (if active) accept the aligned-AI framing?
- Does the red-team/adversarial suite find any way the new wording weakens existing protections?

## 5. Implementation note

This amendment is intentionally separate from the canonical `NORTH_STAR_SSOT.md` file. It should only be merged after the adversarial ratification suite has run and Adam has explicitly ordered ratification.
