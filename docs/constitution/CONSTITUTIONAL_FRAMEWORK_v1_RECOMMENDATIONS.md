# Constitutional Framework v1.0 — Final Recommendations Before Ratification

## Summary

The v1 correction pass has incorporated the feedback from the draft-driven review. The following changes were made and the remaining items must close before the framework can be ratified under the standard: **no known material defect remains unaddressed; every known uncertainty is honestly classified; every foundational promise has an enforcement or accountability path; and the framework contains a disciplined mechanism for learning from future reality.**

## Changes made in this correction pass

1. **Mandatory frame challenge (§8.1).** No independent reasoning may begin without a frame-challenge record that examines embedded assumptions, excluded alternatives, stakeholder framing, and whether "do nothing," "defer," "experiment," or "reframe" are legitimate options.
2. **Founder Authority Under Constitutional Scrutiny (§12).** Formalized retained powers, powers the founder cannot exercise deceptively, consensus as default, founder-directed decisions without full consensus, the three acts (constitutional action, founder exception, constitutional amendment), reality as final arbiter, Digital Twin safeguards, and emergency/succession disclosure requirements.
3. **Amendment classes and anti-self-weakening (§8.2).** Defined ordinary, protected-clause, and meta-amendment requirements; added sequential approval, the lock-may-not-be-removed-before-the-door rule, and explicit asymmetry (weakening protections requires the highest burden; strengthening may use a lower but still high threshold).
4. **Minimum viable remedy (§8.11).** Added accessible complaint channel, acknowledgment, conflict-free investigation, pause power, response deadline, appeal path, anti-retaliation, record, correction/restitution, and institutional-learning entry. No product launches without a functional grievance path.
5. **User steering objection path (§14).** Added a protected counter-metric for user-perceived autonomy and a review trigger for steering objections.
6. **Behavior-level verification (§11).** Added the principle that shape-level evidence may establish existence, but only behavior-level evidence may establish enforcement.
7. **Sunset default for temporary measures (§15).** Clarified that the default outcome for a missed review is sunset or rollback unless an explicit extension is approved.
8. **Metric integrity as a function, not an office (§6).** Protected the function that no measured entity may define or exclusively evaluate its own metrics; left the office design to Operations.
9. **Textual / procedural / substantive compliance distinction (§13).** Added that compliance with form or procedure does not cure a substantive violation of a higher constitutional principle.
10. **Reclassified unresolved decisions (UNRESOLVED.md).** Marked UD-1, UD-2, UD-4, UD-5, UD-8, UD-9 as ratification blockers; UD-7 as activation threshold; UD-3 and UD-6 as operational design; UD-10 as post-ratification deliverable.
11. **Red-team classification (REDTEAM.md).** Each red-team item now carries a classification: ratification blocker, required before first production use, activation-threshold requirement, operational design, or monitor through case law.

## Remaining ratification blockers

1. **UD-1 — Amendment thresholds.** Define exact supermajority/unanimity thresholds and the evidence standard for "credible harm."
2. **UD-2 — Independent review office.** Charter the Office of Constitutional Review or equivalent.
3. **UD-4 — Founder emergency powers.** Add numeric limits, conditions, duration, and cooling-off review.
4. **UD-5 — Succession mechanism.** Define selection and binding constraints for a successor, or a mandatory trigger to do so.
5. **UD-8 — Runtime parity test.** Build `verify-constitutional-parity` and wire it into `builder:preflight`.
6. **UD-9 — Adversarial ratification suite.** Write and run the adversarial scenarios.

## Required before first meaningful production use

1. **UD-3 — Calibration ledger and scoring rubric.** Scores may not be used to block or permit action until a calibration ledger exists.
2. **UD-6 — Public participation matrix.** Define thresholds for user councils, practitioner panels, public consultation, and grievance evidence.
3. **R-3 — Source-diversity check for the council.** Add model-family, prompt-framing, discipline, and affected-party diversity checks.
4. **R-5 — Product-governance inheritance enforcement.** Mechanical check in preflight that no product `GOVERNANCE.md` contradicts higher authority.
5. **R-6 — Confidence-score labeling.** Require calibration ledger and uncertainty ranges before scores are acted on.
6. **R-10 — Staged remedy implementation plan.** Define funding, staffing, and accessibility triggers.
7. **R-13 — Steering objection mechanism.** Build the user-facing path and `user-perceived autonomy` counter-metric.
8. **R-18 — Behavior-level verification universalized.** Add `function_behavior_test` assertions to every protected artifact proof.

## Activation-threshold requirements

1. **UD-7 — Legal entity and fiduciary form.** Decide corporate form and legally enforceable provisions when triggers are crossed.
2. **R-12 — Legal embodiment trigger table.** Define exact scale/impact/risk/funding/user thresholds and the obligation to review when any is crossed.
3. **R-17 — Public-participation impact statement.** Require adoption/rejection/why for affected-party input.

## Operational design (not ratification blockers)

1. **R-1 — Emergency budget numeric limits.** (Covered by UD-4 resolution.)
2. **R-2 / R-14 / R-15 — Metric integrity function, Goodhart protection, Chair scorecard.** Implement through Operations as the organization grows.
3. **R-19 — Governance-cost metric.** Track governance effort against decision impact.

## Recommended final steps

1. Resolve the six ratification blockers above.
2. Run the adversarial ratification suite and record outcomes.
3. Produce a ratification decision record with dissent escrow, reopening triggers, and the unresolved items explicitly accepted or deferred.
4. Draft the Public Constitution v1.0 from the ratified framework.
5. Do not ratify until the ratification standard is met item by item.
