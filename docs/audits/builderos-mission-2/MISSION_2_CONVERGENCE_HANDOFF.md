## Decisions made

1. **Grounding gate behavior: FAIL CLOSED FOR SEALING, NOT FACTORY-WIDE.** A deterministic defect (missing named export, nonexistent SQL table, rejected content hash) prevents seal, but the factory may continue around the blocked step. (DECISION-0001.md §1)
2. **Grounding helper location: `services/blueprint-grounding-check.js`.** Authorized as GAP-FILL governance infrastructure. (DECISION-0001.md §2)
3. **WP0.4 was a hard Phase 0 stop-gate.** The overwrite chain was traced to `run-step.js` writing before the rejected-hash check; the narrow fix blocks the write before the filesystem. (DECISION-0001.md §3)
4. **Decision-log schema: hybrid explicit + JSONB.** Stable lifecycle fields are columns; per-role reasoning and alternatives live in JSONB `metadata`. (DECISION-0001.md §4)
5. **Revenue loop deferred until blueprint authority is proven.** SMOS revenue remains a protected Mission 2 lane but does not execute until the authority spine is proven. (DECISION-0001.md §5)

## Unresolved questions / known gaps

3. The overwrite guard blocks identical bytes; it does not yet block semantically equivalent but textually different regenerated defects.

5. Revenue loop and SMOS live charge remain unproven.
