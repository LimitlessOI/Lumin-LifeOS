# Creative Brief — Game Publisher (game-publisher)

## Creative Brief: Game Publisher

### 1. One-sentence promise
You become a game creator, instantly bringing your ideas to life as playable browser games.

### 2. Tagline options
*   Your Idea. Playable. Now.
*   Create. Play. Share. Instantly.
*   The Fastest Way to Game.

### 3. Key messages
*   **From Idea to Play in Minutes:** Transform any game concept into a playable browser game with a simple text description.
*   **No Code, No Hassle:** Generate complete, ready-to-play HTML/JS games without a single line of code or complex build steps.
*   **Unleash Your Inner Game Designer:** Experiment, iterate, and share your unique game visions effortlessly.
*   **Play Anywhere, Share Everywhere:** Your games are pure web files, easily accessible and shareable via a simple URL.

### 4. Visual direction
*   **Color:** Vibrant, energetic, and playful. Think bright blues, greens, and purples, evoking creativity and digital fun. Avoid overly corporate or dark tones.
*   **Tone:** Empowering, magical, accessible, and exciting. Focus on the joy of creation and instant gratification.
*   **Imagery:** Dynamic screenshots of diverse, simple browser games (platformers, clickers). Visuals of text prompts transforming into game worlds. Abstract representations of AI creativity. Focus on the 'before and after' – a simple idea becoming a rich experience.

### 5. Content formats recommended
*   **Short Video (30-60s):** Demonstrating the 'idea to play in under 2 minutes' flow.
*   **Interactive Landing Page:** Featuring a live demo or a 'try it now' prompt, showcasing generated games.
*   **Social Media Graphics/GIFs:** Highlighting quick game generation and diverse game types.
*   **Email Series:** Onboarding new users, showcasing game ideas, and sharing tips.
*   **Blog Posts:** Featuring user-generated games, creative prompts, and the magic behind the AI.

### 6. Launch narrative arc
*   **Hook:** "Ever dreamed of making a game, but stopped at the first line of code?" (Problem/Aspiration)
*   **Proof:** "Now, simply describe your game idea, and watch as AI brings it to life as a playable browser game in under 2 minutes. No coding, no downloads, just pure play." (Solution/Demonstration)
*   **Call to Action:** "Start creating your first game today. What will you build?" (Empowerment/Invitation)

### 7. Creative asset backlog
1.  **Hero Video:** 30-second demo showing text prompt -> game generation -> gameplay.
2.  **Landing Page Copy & Wireframe:** Focused on the 'under 2 minutes' promise and a clear CTA.
3.  **Social Media Ad Set (3-5 variations):** Short video clips/GIFs with taglines.
4.  **Email Welcome Series (3 emails):** Introducing the product, showcasing examples, and encouraging first game creation.
5.  **Brand Style Guide Snippet:** Defining core colors, fonts, and imagery for consistency.

### 8. Confidence and the biggest unknown that would sharpen the brief
**Confidence:** High. The core promise is clear and compelling.
**Biggest Unknown:** The actual quality and complexity of games generated by `gpt-4o` at 4096 tokens. Knowing the typical 'wow factor' of a generated game would allow for more precise messaging around game depth and replayability.
