# Creative Brief — Conflict Arbitrator (conflict-arbitrator)

**Product:** Conflict Arbitrator  
**Lens:** Creative Director  
**Responsibility:** creative  
**Confidence:** 0.9

## One-sentence promise
The Conflict Arbitrator empowers individuals to navigate difficult conversations with clarity and fairness, transforming moments of tension into opportunities for understanding.

## Position
This product offers a profound shift in how people approach conflict, moving from avoidance or escalation to guided resolution. The creative challenge is to convey its immediate utility and the deep sense of relief and control it provides, all while emphasizing its non-invasive, private nature. The story must be about empowerment and peace of mind, not just a tool.

## Knowledge this brief draws on
- Product works as an overlay on existing communication platforms.
- Multi-model AI ensures fairness and prevents single-AI bias.
- Local-first privacy is a core differentiator for sensitive conflict data.

## Judgment (creative recommendation)
The creative direction should focus on the immediate, in-the-moment relief and the promise of fair, private resolution. Visuals and messaging need to be calming, clear, and reassuring, directly addressing the anxiety associated with conflict. The narrative must highlight the 'anywhere, anytime' accessibility and the integrity of the multi-model approach.

## Evidence
- Overlay architecture directly enables 'anywhere, anytime' positioning.
- Multi-model consensus is a strong integrity guarantee that needs to be communicated simply.
- Local-first privacy is a critical trust-builder for sensitive data.

## Disagreements to resolve
- cfo-roi: The initial creative investment in establishing trust and emotional connection is paramount for a product dealing with such sensitive user states, even if immediate ROI metrics are hard to quantify.
- toyota-lean: Polished, emotionally resonant creative assets are not 'waste' but essential for conveying the product's value and building user trust in a sensitive domain. Iteration on the story is critical before implementation.

## Recommended next action
Develop a comprehensive visual and tonal style guide that embodies calm, clarity, and trust for the Conflict Arbitrator.
