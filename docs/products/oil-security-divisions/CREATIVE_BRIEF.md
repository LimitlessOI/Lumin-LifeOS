# Creative Brief — Oil Security Divisions (oil-security-divisions)

```markdown
## Creative Brief: OIL Security Divisions

### 1. One-sentence promise
Builders gain verifiable, integrated security confidence, proving every fix with receipts, without sacrificing autonomy or budget.

### 2. Tagline options
*   OIL Security: Prove It.
*   Receipt-Native Security: Your Proof, Your Control.
*   Builder Security: Verified, Not Vague.

### 3. Key messages
*   **Security is integrated, not an interruption:** Seamlessly woven into Builder workflows, not an external gate.
*   **Every fix is proven:** Security truth is only canon when independently verified with a receipt.
*   **Empowerment through transparency:** Understand and control your security posture with clear, auditable proof.
*   **Practical protection, not theater:** Focused on real risks and verifiable solutions, avoiding unnecessary complexity.

### 4. Visual direction
*   **Color:** Clean, professional palette with accents of deep blue (trust, stability) and a subtle, almost clinical green (verification, safety). Avoid aggressive reds or yellows.
*   **Tone:** Confident, precise, transparent, empowering, and understated. Avoid alarmist, overly technical, or 'hacker' aesthetics. Focus on clarity and control.
*   **Imagery:** Abstract representations of data flow, interlocking systems, clear dashboards, and subtle visual cues of verification (e.g., checkmarks, digital signatures, receipt-like elements). Focus on clean lines, modern typography, and a sense of order.

### 5. Content formats recommended
*   **Short explainer video (90-120 seconds):** Animated, illustrating the 'find→fix→verify→close' loop with receipt proof.
*   **Landing page:** Dedicated page explaining the three lanes, the receipt-native approach, and Builder benefits.
*   **Infographic:** Visualizing the `security_receipts` flow and the 'truth is not canon until verified' principle.
*   **Email series (3-part):** Introducing the concept, detailing the 'how it works,' and showcasing the benefits.
*   **Social media graphics:** Bite-sized messages and visuals for key principles.

### 6. Launch narrative arc
*   **Hook:** "Is your security truly secure, or just assumed?" – Highlighting the common blind spots and the need for verifiable truth.
*   **Proof:** Introduce OIL Security Divisions as the answer, emphasizing the receipt-native approach and the 'find→fix→verify→close' loop. Showcase how receipts provide undeniable proof.
*   **Call to Action:** "Gain verifiable security confidence. Explore OIL Security Divisions and integrate proof into your Builder workflow." – Directing users to the landing page for more details and integration steps.

### 7. Creative asset backlog
1.  **Landing Page Copy & Wireframe:** (High Priority)
2.  **Explainer Video Script & Storyboard:** (High Priority)
3.  **Key Visuals/Illustrations for Landing Page:** (Medium Priority)
4.  **Email Series Copy:** (Medium Priority)
5.  **Social Media Snippets & Graphics:** (Low Priority)

### 8. Confidence and the biggest unknown
**Confidence:** High.
**Biggest unknown:** The specific 'look and feel' of the `security_receipts` themselves. Understanding their visual representation will significantly sharpen the overall visual direction and content strategy.
```
