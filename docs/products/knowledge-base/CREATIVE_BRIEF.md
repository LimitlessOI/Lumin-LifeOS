# Creative Brief — Knowledge Base (knowledge-base)

**Product:** Knowledge Base  
**Lens:** Creative Director  
**Responsibility:** creative  
**Confidence:** 0.85

## One-sentence promise
The Knowledge Base empowers AI with tailored expertise and real-time web awareness, transforming it into an indispensable, context-rich partner.

## Position
This product is about elevating AI from a general tool to a specialized, informed collaborator. The creative focus must be on the transformation of AI output and the user's enhanced capability, not just the underlying data ingestion. We are selling intelligence, not just data.

## Knowledge this brief draws on
- Product provides 'user-uploadable knowledge base that injects domain context into AI responses.'
- Product offers 'Web intelligence tools — scraping, competitor analysis, marketing research, and web search.'
- Mission: 'Give the AI council domain expertise it wasn't trained on, and real-time awareness of the web.'

## Judgment (creative recommendation)
The creative direction should emphasize the 'smart' aspect of the AI, powered by this knowledge. Visuals should convey clarity, insight, and seamless integration. The narrative needs to highlight the 'before and after' of AI performance, positioning the Knowledge Base as the essential upgrade for any AI-driven project.

## Evidence
- The product's core function is to 'inject domain context into AI responses,' directly impacting AI quality.
- The 'Mission' explicitly states giving AI 'domain expertise' and 'real-time awareness.'
- Revenue model links directly to 'better AI output quality across all projects.'

## Disagreements to resolve
- technical-architect: The technical details of vector search or specific API providers, while important for implementation, should not dictate the customer-facing story. The creative focus is on the outcome, not the mechanism.
- cfo-roi: While indirect revenue is noted, the immediate creative goal is to articulate the value proposition and emotional resonance, which may not always have a direct, immediate ROI metric.

## Recommended next action
Develop the core promise statement and tagline options to articulate the transformation this product offers to AI users.
