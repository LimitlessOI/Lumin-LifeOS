# Creative Brief — Video Pipeline (video-pipeline)

## Creative Brief: Video Pipeline

### 1. One-sentence promise
You become a cinematic storyteller, bringing any script to life as a publishable video, effortlessly and without a camera or editing software.

### 2. Tagline options
*   **Your Script. Your Cinema.**
*   **From Text to Take.**
*   **Unleash Your Vision. Instantly.**

### 3. Key messages
*   Transform any text script into a high-quality, publishable video.
*   Bypass traditional production: no camera, no complex editing software required.
*   Choose between speed or cinematic fidelity with advanced AI models.
*   Streamline your content creation, from concept to YouTube-ready video.
*   See your cost upfront, ensuring full control before you create.

### 4. Visual direction
*   **Color:** Deep, rich blues and purples with bright, energetic accents (e.g., electric teal, vibrant gold) to convey sophistication and innovation.
*   **Tone:** Empowering, modern, accessible, and slightly futuristic. Focus on the magic of creation.
*   **Imagery:** Abstract, flowing light patterns; subtle, dynamic motion graphics; clean, minimalist UI mockups; examples of diverse, high-quality AI-generated video scenes (landscapes, abstract concepts, stylized characters – *never real people without consent*). Emphasize transformation and fluidity.

### 5. Content formats recommended
*   **Short Video:** Animated explainers (30-60s) demonstrating the text-to-video flow.
*   **Graphic:** Infographics illustrating the simplicity of the 6-step pipeline.
*   **Landing Page:** Dedicated page with clear promise, examples, and call to action.
*   **Email:** Onboarding series showcasing capabilities and tips.
*   **Social Media Snippets:** Quick, engaging clips of generated videos with compelling text overlays.
*   **Case Studies/Showcases:** Examples of diverse video outputs for different use cases (e.g., marketing, education, entertainment).

### 6. Launch narrative arc
*   **Hook:** "Imagine turning any idea into a stunning video, instantly. No cameras. No complex software." (Focus on the pain point of traditional video production).
*   **Proof:** "Introducing Video Pipeline: the revolutionary platform that transforms your scripts into cinematic-quality videos with advanced AI. See how easy it is to go from text to a publishable masterpiece." (Showcase the simplicity and quality with a compelling demo).
*   **Call to Action:** "Start creating your next viral video today. Get your first video generated free/at a special introductory rate." (Direct, clear next step).

### 7. Creative asset backlog
1.  **Hero Video (60s):** Animated explainer demonstrating the full text-to-video process, highlighting key benefits and showing diverse output examples.
2.  **Landing Page Copy & Wireframe:** Core messaging, feature highlights, and clear CTAs.
3.  **Visual Style Guide:** Detailed color palette, typography, iconography, and imagery examples.
4.  **Social Media Teaser Graphics (3-5):** Short, punchy visuals with taglines and a link.
5.  **Email Onboarding Sequence (3 emails):** Welcome, how-to-get-started, advanced tips.

### 8. Confidence and biggest unknown
**Confidence:** High. The product's core mission is clear and directly supports a compelling creative narrative.
**Biggest Unknown:** The actual visual quality and stylistic range of the AI-generated videos (Kling 1.6, Wan 2.1, Flux Schnell) in practice. Seeing concrete, diverse examples would significantly sharpen the visual direction and promise of 'cinematic quality.'
