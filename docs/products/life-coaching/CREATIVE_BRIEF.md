# Creative Brief — Life Coaching (life-coaching)

```markdown
## Creative Brief: Life Coaching

### 1. One-sentence promise
You become the intentional architect of your life, closing the gap between who you are and who you aspire to be.

### 2. Tagline options
*   **Live Your Intent.**
*   **Your Life, Designed.**
*   **The Intentional You, Unlocked.**

### 3. Key messages
*   **Bridge the Gap:** Seamlessly connect your daily actions to your long-term aspirations.
*   **Clarity in Action:** Transform vague desires into concrete goals and track your progress with ease.
*   **Personalized Growth:** Experience a system that adapts to your unique journey, guiding you toward your best self.
*   **Frictionless Intent:** Remove the obstacles to living a life aligned with your deepest values.

### 4. Visual direction
*   **Color:** Soft, inviting gradients (e.g., sunrise/sunset hues, calming blues/greens transitioning to warm golds/oranges). Avoid harsh, corporate tones.
*   **Tone:** Empowering, supportive, reflective, aspirational, calm, and clear. Not overly energetic or demanding.
*   **Imagery:** Clean, minimalist, and evocative. Focus on open spaces, natural light, individuals in thoughtful or active states (e.g., journaling, meditating, looking at a horizon, a clear path ahead). Abstract representations of growth, connection, and clarity. Avoid cluttered or overly literal depictions of technology.

### 5. Content formats recommended
*   **Short Video (60-90 seconds):** An emotional narrative showcasing the 'before and after' of intentional living.
*   **Landing Page:** Comprehensive overview, promise statement, key benefits, social proof (if available), and clear call to action.
*   **Email Sequence (3-5 emails):** Onboarding new users, highlighting core features through the lens of personal growth.
*   **Social Graphics (static & animated):** Bite-sized messages, inspirational quotes, and quick tips related to intentional living.
*   **Interactive Quiz/Assessment:** 'What kind of intentional living architect are you?' to engage potential users.

### 6. Launch narrative arc
*   **Hook:** "Are you living the life you truly intend?" – Address the common feeling of disconnect between aspirations and daily reality.
*   **Proof:** Introduce the Life Coaching product as the solution, demonstrating how it helps users define, track, and achieve their personal goals through a supportive system. Show, don't just tell, the transformation.
*   **Call to Action:** "Start building your intentional life today." – Invite users to experience the platform and begin their journey of self-actualization.

### 7. Creative asset backlog
1.  **Landing Page Copy & Wireframe:** Core messaging and user flow.
2.  **Brand Style Guide:** Define color palette, typography, imagery guidelines, and tone of voice.
3.  **Launch Video Script & Storyboard:** Outline the emotional narrative.
4.  **Core Tagline & Key Message Graphics:** For social media and initial marketing.
5.  **Onboarding Email Sequence Copy:** Guiding new users through the initial experience.

### 8. Confidence and the biggest unknown
**Confidence:** High
**Biggest Unknown:** The specific emotional triggers and pain points of the target user that would allow for even more precise messaging and visual resonance. Understanding their current frustrations with 'unintentional living' would sharpen the 'before' state in the narrative.
```
