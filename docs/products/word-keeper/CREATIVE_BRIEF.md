# Creative Brief — Word Keeper (word-keeper)

**Product:** Word Keeper  
**Lens:** Creative Director  
**Responsibility:** creative  
**Confidence:** 0.9

## One-sentence promise
Word Keeper transforms the abstract concept of integrity into a tangible, actionable practice, empowering users to build a stronger, more reliable self.

## Position
The core challenge is to translate the technical process of ambient listening and AI detection into a deeply personal and empowering experience. This product isn't just about tracking words; it's about cultivating self-trust and demonstrating reliability. The creative direction must emphasize personal growth, accountability, and the quiet strength of keeping one's word, making the technology feel like a supportive partner rather than a surveillance tool.

## Knowledge this brief draws on
- Product context highlights 'integrity is not a trait — it is a practice' and 'the most important word you will ever give is the one you give yourself.'
- Core features include real-time commitment detection, tracking, reminders, scoring, and coaching for honorable exits.
- Legal foundation emphasizes user consent and privacy (NV one-party consent, phone call auto-pause, data encryption).

## Judgment (creative recommendation)
The creative brief should focus on the emotional outcome of using Word Keeper: becoming a person of their word. The visual and tonal direction needs to be sophisticated and reassuring, avoiding any 'big brother' connotations. Content should tell a story of personal transformation and reliability, making the technical aspects secondary to the user's journey of self-mastery. The launch narrative should immediately establish trust and highlight the profound personal benefit.

## Evidence
- The product's foundational statement: 'The most important word you will ever give is the one you give yourself.'
- The explicit mention of 'Integrity Score' and 'Coaches — how to back out with honour' points to a strong personal development angle.
- The detailed legal foundation and consent mechanisms provide a strong basis for building trust in the creative messaging.

## Disagreements to resolve
- cfo-roi: The initial creative investment in establishing trust and emotional connection is paramount, even if immediate ROI metrics are hard to quantify for brand building.
- toyota-lean: Iterative creative exploration for the right tone and visual language is essential to distill intent, and shouldn't be seen as 'waste' before the core story resonates.
- competition: Focusing on the unique emotional and personal growth aspect of Word Keeper is more critical than a feature-by-feature comparison, as the core value proposition is distinct.

## Recommended next action
Develop a comprehensive mood board and tone-of-voice guide that embodies 'quiet strength,' 'personal mastery,' and 'unwavering support' for Word Keeper.
