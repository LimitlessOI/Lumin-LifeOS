# Creative Brief — Ai Receptionist (ai-receptionist)

**Product:** Ai Receptionist  
**Lens:** Creative Director  
**Responsibility:** creative  
**Confidence:** 0.82

## One-sentence promise
The AI Receptionist transforms small business owners into always-on, perfectly present professionals, effortlessly managing inbound calls.

## Position
This product offers a clear, immediate value proposition: reclaiming time and professionalizing call handling. The creative strategy must emphasize the relief and empowerment it provides, positioning the AI not as a replacement, but as an extension of the business owner's best self. The narrative should focus on the outcome for the customer, not just the features.

## Knowledge this brief draws on
- Product targets small businesses, starting with real estate agents.
- Core features include personalized tone matching, call summaries, and scheduling integration.
- Pricing is $99/month SaaS with a 7-day free trial and self-serve checkout.

## Judgment (creative recommendation)
The creative direction should center on the idea of 'effortless professionalism' and 'uninterrupted focus.' Visuals should be clean, modern, and convey a sense of calm and efficiency. The messaging needs to be direct, highlighting the immediate benefits of missed calls becoming qualified leads and reclaimed time. The launch narrative should quickly establish the problem and present the AI Receptionist as the elegant solution.

## Evidence
- The product description clearly outlines the pain points it solves (missing calls, qualifying leads).
- The 'personalized tone matching' and 'scheduling integration' speak to a sophisticated, tailored solution.
- The self-serve, immediate utility model requires a clear, compelling customer-facing story from the outset.

## Disagreements to resolve
- cfo-roi: The creative ambition for high-quality visual and narrative assets might initially seem costly, but it's essential for conveying the premium, professional feel that justifies the $99/month price point and drives self-serve adoption.
- toyota-lean: While rapid iteration is valuable, the initial customer-facing experience (landing page, onboarding flow) needs a polished, complete story to build trust and convert users without discovery calls. This isn't 'waste' but foundational brand building.

## Recommended next action
Develop the core landing page copy and visual mood board to establish the brand's emotional resonance and promise.
