# Creative Brief — Capability Map (capability-map)

## Creative Brief: Capability Map

### 1. One-sentence promise
You become the architect of intentional growth, confidently integrating every innovation into a cohesive, future-proof product.

### 2. Tagline options
*   Capability Map: Build with Purpose.
*   Capability Map: Your Product, Reimagined.
*   Capability Map: Precision Product Evolution.

### 3. Key messages
*   **Strategic Clarity:** Every idea finds its rightful place, ensuring your product evolves with intent, not just accumulation.
*   **Architectural Integrity:** Prevent ad-hoc feature absorption and maintain a clean, logical product structure.
*   **Governed Innovation:** Introduce new capabilities with confidence, knowing they align with your overarching vision.
*   **Future-Proof Your Product:** Build a resilient, adaptable product that can scale and innovate without breaking.
*   **Truth Over Convenience:** Make informed decisions based on a complete understanding of your product's architecture.

### 4. Visual direction
**Color:** A palette of deep blues, cool grays, and crisp whites, with an accent of a vibrant, intelligent green or electric blue to signify insight and growth.
**Tone:** Professional, intelligent, precise, empowering, and forward-thinking. Avoid overly playful or corporate jargon.
**Imagery:** Clean, architectural lines; interconnected nodes or systems; abstract representations of data flow and analysis; images suggesting clarity, order, and strategic oversight (e.g., a blueprint coming into focus, a complex system simplified). Minimalist and modern.

### 5. Content formats recommended
*   **Short Explainer Video (90-120 seconds):** Animated, demonstrating the flow from idea submission to mapping and integration, highlighting the 'before and after' of using the Capability Map.
*   **Infographic/Graphic:** Visualizing the analysis flow and the different mapping types (existing module, extension point, new segment).
*   **Landing Page:** Dedicated page with problem/solution framing, key benefits, a clear call to action, and a demo video embed.
*   **Email Series (3-part):** Introducing the problem, presenting the solution, and showcasing the benefits with a clear CTA.
*   **Product Tour/Interactive Demo:** A guided walkthrough of the Command Center panel for surfacing pending capabilities.

### 6. Launch narrative arc
*   **Hook:** "Is your product growing, or just getting bigger? The hidden cost of ad-hoc features." (Focus on the pain of architectural debt and feature sprawl.)
*   **Proof:** "Introducing the Capability Map: The intelligent system that brings order to your product's evolution. See how every idea is analyzed, mapped, and integrated with precision, ensuring your product grows strategically." (Showcase the analysis flow and mapping decisions.)
*   **Call to Action:** "Stop building in the dark. Start building with purpose. Explore the Capability Map and transform your product's future." (Direct to a demo or sign-up.)

### 8. Confidence and the biggest unknown
**Confidence:** High. The product's mission is clear, and the problem it solves is tangible for product builders.
**Biggest Unknown:** The specific user persona's emotional connection to 'architectural integrity' versus 'speed of shipping.' Understanding if the primary user values long-term structural health over immediate feature delivery will sharpen the messaging around the 'truth over convenience' anchor.
