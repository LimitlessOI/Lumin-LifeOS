# Creative Brief — Outreach Crm (outreach-crm)

## Creative Brief: Outreach CRM

### 1. One-sentence promise
You become the trusted connector, building relationships with precision and peace of mind through compliant, automated outreach.

### 2. Tagline options
*   Outreach CRM: Connect with Confidence.
*   Smart Outreach, Ethical Engagement.
*   Your Audience, Your Rules, Our Automation.

### 3. Key messages
*   **Effortless Compliance:** Automate multi-channel outreach with built-in, fail-closed consent enforcement, ensuring every message is sent ethically.
*   **Targeted Connections:** Deliver the right message, to the right person, at the right time, across email, SMS, and calls.
*   **Streamlined Follow-Up:** Manage prospect and client sequences with ease, integrating seamlessly with your existing CRM (e.g., BoldTrail).
*   **Audit-Ready Transparency:** Maintain a complete, unalterable log of all communications and consent actions for total peace of mind.

### 4. Visual direction
*   **Color:** Clean, professional palette with blues (trust, reliability) and greens (growth, ethics). Accents of a warm, inviting color to convey human connection.
*   **Tone:** Confident, intelligent, reassuring, and efficient. Avoid overly aggressive or 'salesy' language. Focus on empowerment and control.
*   **Imagery:** Clean UI screenshots, subtle animations showing message flow, diverse people connecting (not just screens), abstract representations of data flow and consent gates. Avoid stock photos that feel generic or overly staged. Emphasize clarity and order.

### 5. Content formats recommended
*   **Landing Page:** Primary conversion point, detailing benefits, features, and a clear call to action.
*   **Short Explainer Video (90-120 seconds):** Animated or live-action, demonstrating the core value proposition and ease of use, with a focus on consent.
*   **Feature-focused Graphics/Infographics:** Visually explain the consent model, sequence management, and multi-channel capabilities.
*   **Email Drip Campaign:** For onboarding and feature announcements, showcasing the product's own capabilities.
*   **Social Media Snippets:** Short, punchy messages and visuals highlighting compliance and efficiency.
*   **Case Studies/Testimonials:** (Once available) demonstrating real-world success and peace of mind.

### 6. Launch narrative arc
*   **Hook:** Are you tired of the compliance tightrope walk in outreach? Worried about accidental missteps and missed connections?
*   **Proof:** Introduce Outreach CRM – the intelligent platform that automates your multi-channel campaigns while rigorously enforcing consent, every single time. Show how it simplifies complex rules and ensures ethical engagement.
*   **Call to Action:** Start connecting with confidence. Get started with Outreach CRM today and transform your prospect follow-up.

### 7. Creative asset backlog
1.  **Landing Page Copy & Wireframe:** (High Priority)
2.  **Explainer Video Script & Storyboard:** (High Priority)
3.  **Core UI Mockups/Screenshots:** (Medium Priority)
4.  **Tagline & Key Message Graphics:** (Medium Priority)
5.  **Social Media Launch Kit (5-7 posts):** (Medium Priority)
6.  **Email Onboarding Sequence (3 emails):** (Low Priority)

### 8. Confidence and the biggest unknown
**Confidence:** High
**Biggest Unknown:** The specific pain points and language used by the target user (e.g., real estate agents using BoldTrail) regarding consent and outreach. Sharpening this would allow for more resonant messaging.
