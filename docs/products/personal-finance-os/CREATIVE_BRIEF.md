# Creative Brief — Personal Finance Os (personal-finance-os)

```markdown
## Creative Brief: Personal Finance OS

### 1. One-sentence promise
You become the clear-eyed author of your financial story, empowered by truth, not judgment.

### 2. Tagline options
*   Personal Finance OS: See Your Money, Own Your Choices.
*   Your Money, Unfiltered. Your Future, Defined.
*   The Financial Mirror. Your Truth.

### 3. Key messages
*   **Clarity, Not Judgment:** Understand where your money goes without shame or guilt.
*   **Your Choices, Supported:** [unverified — no system query ran] you what is, so you can decide what's next.
*   **Beyond the Budget:** Adapt your financial structure to your life's seasons and events.
*   **Build True Resilience:** See your financial runway and build security on your terms.

### 4. Visual direction
*   **Color:** Clean, calming palette. Soft blues, greens, and muted earth tones. Avoid aggressive reds or overly bright, 'gamified' colors. Subtle gradients for depth.
*   **Tone:** Calm, insightful, empowering, reflective, honest, supportive, non-judgmental. Avoid urgency, scarcity, or aspirational wealth imagery.
*   **Imagery:** Abstract representations of clarity, flow, and connection (e.g., light through water, interconnected lines, calm landscapes). Data visualizations should be elegant and easy to interpret, not overwhelming. Focus on diverse, authentic individuals engaged in thoughtful reflection or making deliberate choices, not just smiling at a screen.

### 5. Content formats recommended
*   **Landing Page:** Core narrative, feature highlights, clear value proposition.
*   **Short Explainer Videos:** Animated sequences demonstrating 'Cashflow Truth' and 'Budget Modes' with a focus on the emotional benefit of clarity.
*   **Educational Blog/Resource Articles:** Deep dives into financial concepts (e.g., 'Understanding Your Fixed Obligations,' 'The Psychology of Discretionary Spending') framed through the OS's lens of clarity.
*   **Social Media Graphics:** Shareable insights, questions for self-reflection, and simple data visualizations.
*   **Email Onboarding Series:** Guiding users through initial setup, emphasizing the 'mirror-first' philosophy.
*   **In-App Microcopy:** Reinforce the non-judgmental, empowering tone throughout the user journey.

### 6. Launch narrative arc
*   **Hook:** "Tired of financial apps telling you what to do? What if you could simply *see* your money, clearly and without judgment?" (Addresses pain point of prescriptive advice)
*   **Proof:** "Introducing Personal Finance OS: the system built to reflect your financial reality, not dictate it. Discover where your money truly goes, understand your patterns, and choose your path forward with confidence. From 'Cashflow Truth' to flexible 'Budget Modes,' we provide the clarity, you make the decisions." (Introduces product, highlights key features as enablers of clarity)
*   **Call to Action:** "Start your journey to financial clarity. Get Personal Finance OS today and author your own financial story." (Empowering, action-oriented, aligns with core promise)

### 7. Creative asset backlog
1.  **Landing Page Copy & Wireframe:** Core narrative, feature sections, CTAs.
2.  **Brand Style Guide:** Define color palette, typography, imagery guidelines, and tone of voice.
3.  **Explainer Video Script & Storyboard (Cashflow Truth):** Focus on the 'seeing clearly' benefit.
4.  **Social Media Launch Kit:** 5-7 graphics/short videos for initial awareness.
5.  **Onboarding Email Sequence Copy:** 3-part series introducing the OS philosophy and setup.

### 8. Confidence and biggest unknown
**Confidence:** High. The product's mission and constraints provide a very clear creative direction, emphasizing self-discovery and clarity over traditional financial app tropes.

**Biggest Unknown:** The specific emotional signals from LifeOS for 'Stress spend detection.' Understanding the *nature* of these signals (e.g., HRV, joy score) will be crucial for designing how this information is presented to the user in a way that is informative and supportive, never accusatory or shaming.
```
