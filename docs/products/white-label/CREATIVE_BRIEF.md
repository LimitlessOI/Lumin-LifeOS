# Creative Brief — White Label (white-label)

```markdown
### Creative Brief: White Label Product

1.  **One-sentence promise:** Partners become the definitive AI solution for their clients, powered by our invisible, enterprise-grade engine.

2.  **Tagline options:**
    *   Your Brand. Their AI. Our Engine.
    *   Unleash Your AI. Seamlessly Branded.
    *   The Future of AI, Under Your Flag.

3.  **Key messages:**
    *   **Elevate Your Brand:** Offer a complete AI platform under your own name, instantly boosting your market position.
    *   **Expand Your Services:** Provide cutting-edge AI council, outreach, CRM, and coaching without the development burden.
    *   **Scale Your Revenue:** Tap into a powerful, recurring revenue stream by selling a proven AI solution to your client base.
    *   **Focus on Relationships:** We handle the infrastructure; you own the client experience and strategic growth.
    *   **Seamless Integration:** Custom domains, logos, and a fully branded experience make it truly yours.

4.  **Visual direction:**
    *   **Color:** Sophisticated, clean, and professional. Think deep blues, silvers, and whites, with subtle accents of a vibrant, trustworthy color (e.g., a rich teal or deep purple) to signify innovation. Avoid overly bright or playful palettes.
    *   **Tone:** Authoritative, empowering, forward-thinking, and reliable. Emphasize partnership and growth.
    *   **Imagery:** Clean lines, abstract representations of data flow and connectivity, professional and diverse business people collaborating, sleek interfaces with partner branding. Focus on the *outcome* for the partner (growth, client satisfaction) rather than just the technology itself. Avoid overly technical or complex visuals.

5.  **Content formats recommended:**
    *   **Landing Page:** Primary conversion point, detailing benefits, features, and a clear call to action.
    *   **Short Explainer Video (90-120 seconds):** Visually demonstrate the white-label experience and partner benefits.
    *   **Partner Case Studies/Testimonials (graphics & short text):** Showcase successful partner transformations.
    *   **Email Sequence:** Nurture leads with deeper dives into specific features and ROI.
    *   **Sales Deck/Brochure (PDF):** Comprehensive overview for direct sales conversations.
    *   **Social Media Graphics (LinkedIn focus):** Highlight key benefits and thought leadership.

6.  **Launch narrative arc:**
    *   **Hook:** "Imagine offering your clients a complete AI platform, fully branded as your own, without building a single line of code." (Problem: The desire to offer advanced AI vs. the cost/complexity of building it.)
    *   **Proof:** "Our White Label solution provides the entire LimitlessOS engine – AI council, CRM, outreach, coaching – seamlessly integrated under your brand, managed by us. See how [Partner X] leveraged this to [achieve Y result]." (Solution: Our platform. Evidence: Partner success.)
    *   **Call to Action:** "Schedule a demo to explore how your brand can become an AI leader. Unlock new revenue streams and deepen client relationships today."

7.  **Creative asset backlog:**
    1.  **White Label Product Landing Page Copy & Wireframe:** (High Priority)
    2.  **Core Explainer Video Script & Storyboard:** (High Priority)
    3.  **Sales Deck/Brochure (PDF) Content & Design:** (Medium Priority)
    4.  **Email Nurture Sequence Copy (3 emails):** (Medium Priority)
    5.  **Social Media Launch Kit (5 graphics + copy):** (Low Priority)

8.  **Confidence:** High. The product's core value proposition is clear. The biggest unknown that would sharpen the brief is **specific target partner profiles and their primary pain points/aspirations**, which would allow for more tailored messaging and visual metaphors.
```
