# Creative Brief — Site Builder (site-builder)

## Creative Brief

### 1. One-sentence promise
Local businesses transform their weak websites into high-converting, premium sales engines that drive immediate financial activity.

### 2. Tagline options
*   Your Business, Elevated. Your Sales, Accelerated.
*   From Stale Site to Sales Machine.
*   The Website That Works For You.

### 3. Key messages
*   **Instant Upgrade:** Get a modern, sales-optimized website preview built from your existing site, effortlessly.
*   **Designed to Convert:** Every site is crafted to book appointments, generate calls, and drive purchases, not just look good.
*   **Premium Look, Professional Results:** Impress your customers with a custom-feel site that reflects your business's quality.
*   **Done-For-You Simplicity:** We handle the tech, hosting, and setup, so you can focus on your business.
*   **Unlock New Business:** Turn passive visitors into active customers with a site engineered for growth.

### 4. Visual direction
*   **Color:** Clean, professional, and trustworthy. A primary palette of deep blues/greens with warm, inviting accents (e.g., a soft gold or terracotta) to convey both reliability and approachability. Avoid overly corporate or overly playful tones.
*   **Tone:** Confident, empowering, efficient, and results-oriented. Empathetic to the small business owner's challenges, but focused on the solution.
*   **Imagery:** Before-and-after comparisons (subtle, implied transformation rather than direct comparison). Images of thriving local businesses (dentists, attorneys, contractors, etc.) interacting positively with customers, alongside clean, modern website interfaces. Focus on the *outcome* for the business owner (e.g., a full appointment book, a busy shop, a handshake closing a deal) rather than just abstract tech. Avoid stock photos that feel generic; aim for authentic, aspirational realism.

### 5. Content formats recommended
*   **Short Video (60-90 seconds):** Animated explainer showing the 'before' (struggling business owner, weak site) to 'after' (thriving business, new site driving activity), highlighting the automated build and conversion focus.
*   **Landing Page:** Dedicated page for the free preview offer, clearly outlining the benefits, process, and showcasing example transformations.
*   **Cold Email Sequence:** Initial email with the free preview link, followed by a nurture sequence addressing common pain points and reinforcing conversion benefits.
*   **Graphic Infographics:** Simple visuals demonstrating the 'AI + layout shells' process and the types of financial activity generated.
*   **Social Media Snippets:** Short, punchy text/image posts highlighting specific benefits (e.g., 'Stop losing leads to a bad website').

### 6. Launch narrative arc
*   **Hook:** "Is your website costing you business? See how a modern, sales-driven site can transform your leads." (Focus on the pain point of a weak site and the promise of a better outcome.)
*   **Proof:** "We've built a free, premium preview of your new site, designed to convert. No coding, no hassle – just results. See it in action." (Showcase the automated build, the quality, and the conversion features.)
*   **Call to Action:** "Claim your upgraded site and start turning visitors into customers. It's time your website worked as hard as you do." (Direct, benefit-driven call to upgrade/activate.)

### 7. Creative asset backlog
1.  **Free Preview Email Template:** High-converting design and copy for the initial cold email.
2.  **Landing Page Copy & Wireframe:** Core messaging and layout for the free preview sign-up/reveal page.
3.  **Brand Style Guide (Mini):** Define core colors, typography, and imagery guidelines for consistency.
4.  **Explainer Video Script & Storyboard:** Outline for the 60-90 second animated video.
5.  **Social Media Ad Concepts (Image/Copy):** Initial ideas for paid social campaigns targeting local businesses.

### 8. Confidence and the biggest unknown that would sharpen the brief
**Confidence:** High

**Biggest Unknown:** The specific, most compelling 'unanswered questions the buyer still has for that industry' (as per 'Start from the client' doctrine) that can be directly addressed by the site builder's multi-path design. Understanding these deeply would allow for more targeted messaging and visual examples in the creative assets.
