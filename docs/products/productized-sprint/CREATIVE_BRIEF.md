# Creative Brief — Productized Sprint (productized-sprint)

```markdown
## Creative Brief: Productized Sprint

### 1. One-sentence promise
You become unstuck, equipped with immediate, expert-crafted solutions that propel your business forward, without the usual friction.

### 2. Tagline options
*   **Your Business, Unstuck. Fast.**
*   **Expert Solutions. Delivered. Now.**
*   **The Shortcut to Done.**

### 3. Key messages
*   **Immediate Impact:** Get critical business assets fixed or built in days, not months.
*   **Fixed Price, Zero Surprises:** Clear pricing means you know exactly what you're getting and what you're paying.
*   **Expert + AI Power:** Leverage Adam's proven expertise amplified by AI for precision and speed.
*   **No-Friction Process:** Skip discovery calls and custom scoping; just pick, pay, and receive.
*   **Tangible Outputs:** Receive ready-to-deploy assets that directly solve your business problems.

### 4. Visual direction
*   **Color:** Clean, modern palette with a primary accent color that conveys urgency and clarity (e.g., a vibrant electric blue or a sharp, confident green). Neutrals should be professional and minimalist (grays, whites).
*   **Tone:** Confident, efficient, direct, empowering, and slightly understated professional. Avoid overly corporate or overly casual. Focus on competence and results.
*   **Imagery:** Clean, uncluttered visuals. Abstract representations of speed, progress, and problem-solving (e.g., a clear path, a lightbulb moment, gears turning smoothly). Minimalist iconography. Avoid stock photos of smiling people; focus on the *outcome* of the work. Think sleek dashboards, simplified workflows, before-and-after comparisons (conceptual).

### 5. Content formats recommended
*   **Landing Page:** Primary hub for all offers, clear hierarchy, direct CTAs.
*   **Short Video (Explainer):** 60-90 seconds, animated or motion graphics, illustrating the problem-solution flow for each offer.
*   **Social Media Graphics:** Quote cards, "before & after" problem/solution snippets, direct offer announcements.
*   **Email Sequence:** Nurture sequence for interested leads, detailing each offer's benefits and process.
*   **Comparison Table/Infographic:** Clearly outlining the differences between the three offers.

### 6. Launch narrative arc
*   **Hook:** "Is something broken in your business? Or do you need something built, fast?" (Focus on the immediate pain point or urgent need).
*   **Proof:** "Introducing the Productized Sprint: expert-level solutions delivered in days, not weeks. See how [Offer 1] fixes your [specific problem] in 24 hours, or how [Offer 2] builds your [specific asset] in 3-5 days." (Showcase the speed, clarity, and tangible results of each offer with brief examples).
*   **Call to Action:** "Stop waiting. Start solving. Choose your sprint and get your business moving today." (Direct, confident call to immediate action).

### 7. Creative asset backlog
1.  **Productized Sprint Landing Page (Wireframe + Core Copy)**
2.  **"Speed Fix" Social Media Ad Set (3 variations: problem-focused, solution-focused, urgency-focused)**
3.  **Explainer Video Script & Storyboard (60-90 seconds)**
4.  **Email Welcome Sequence (3 emails: intro, offer 1 deep dive, offer 2 deep dive)**
5.  **Visual Style Guide (Color palette, typography, imagery examples)**

### 8. Confidence and biggest unknown
**Confidence:** High.
**Biggest unknown:** The specific visual language and iconography that best conveys both "AI-assisted" and "Adam's personal services stack" without feeling generic or overly technical.
```
