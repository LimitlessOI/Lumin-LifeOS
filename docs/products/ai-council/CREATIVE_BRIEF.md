# Creative Brief — Ai Council (ai-council)

**Product:** Ai Council  
**Lens:** Creative Director  
**Responsibility:** creative  
**Confidence:** 0.82

## One-sentence promise
The Ai Council empowers users to build with intelligent efficiency, ensuring every AI call delivers optimal value and quality without compromise.

## Position
The Ai Council is not just a routing layer; it's the intelligent core that transforms AI development from a gamble into a strategic advantage. Our creative approach will focus on translating its technical sophistication into a clear promise of control, quality, and cost-effectiveness, positioning it as an indispensable partner for any platform building with AI.

## Knowledge this brief draws on
- Product's core mission: 'Route every AI task to the best-value model for its role. Quality first, especially on the most important functions.'
- Addresses 'runaway API costs and low-quality output from wrong-model routing.'
- Generates revenue directly through 'API Cost Savings Service'.

## Judgment (creative recommendation)
The creative direction should emphasize the user's newfound control and the tangible benefits of superior AI output and cost savings. The narrative needs to move beyond technical specifications to the emotional impact of reliable, high-quality, and cost-optimized AI operations. This positions the Ai Council as a strategic asset, not just a utility.

## Evidence
- The product directly addresses pain points of cost and quality, which are strong emotional drivers for users.
- The 'council' metaphor itself suggests deliberation, wisdom, and considered decisions, which can be leveraged creatively.
- The 'API Cost Savings Service' highlights a direct, measurable value proposition for external clients.

## Disagreements to resolve
- cfo-roi: While cost savings are a key feature, the creative focus should first establish the value of quality and strategic control, as these often justify initial investment or perceived higher-cost models for critical tasks. We need to sell the 'why' before the 'how much'.
- toyota-lean: The initial creative exploration and development of a compelling narrative and visual world are crucial for market adoption and should not be seen as 'waste.' Polishing the story before implementation ensures the product resonates effectively.
