# Creative Brief — MarketingOS (marketingos)

## Creative Brief: MarketingOS

### 1. One-sentence promise
Founders become authentic content leaders, effortlessly sharing their unique voice and stories to build influence and grow their business.

### 2. Tagline options
*   Your Voice. Amplified.
*   Authentic Content, Unlocked.
*   Speak Your Story. Grow Your Business.

### 3. Key messages
*   **Unleash Your True Voice:** Transform your natural conversations and expertise into compelling content that sounds exactly like you.
*   **Effortless Content Engine:** Go from idea to published content across platforms without writing, editing, or feeling fake.
*   **Story-Driven Growth:** Attract and engage your ideal audience with authentic narratives that build trust and drive results.
*   **Beyond Generic AI:** Get intelligent content tailored to your unique brand, not templated output.

### 4. Visual direction
*   **Color:** Clean, modern palette with a primary focus on deep blues and greens, accented by warm, inviting yellows or oranges. Avoid overly corporate or sterile tones.
*   **Tone:** Authentic, intelligent, empowering, approachable, confident, and clear. Not flashy or overly 'techy.'
*   **Imagery:** Focus on real founders in natural, thoughtful poses (not staged 'influencer' shots). Abstract representations of thought, connection, and growth. Clean UI mockups that emphasize simplicity and clarity. Avoid generic stock photos of people talking into microphones or looking at screens. Emphasize the *founder's* story, not just the tool.

### 5. Content formats recommended
*   **Short Explainer Videos:** Animated or live-action (authentic founder testimonials) demonstrating the core 'speak-to-content' loop.
*   **Landing Page:** Clear, benefit-driven copy with strong calls to action, visual examples of content output, and founder testimonials.
*   **Email Sequences:** Onboarding, educational, and nurturing emails that reinforce the promise of authenticity and ease.
*   **Social Graphics:** Quote cards from founders, simple infographics explaining the process, 'before & after' content examples.
*   **Blog Posts/Articles:** Deep dives into the philosophy of authentic marketing, case studies, and 'how-to' guides for using the system.
*   **Audio Snippets:** Short audio clips demonstrating the 'interview' process and the clarity of the extracted voice.

### 6. Launch narrative arc
*   **Hook:** "Tired of feeling fake on camera or struggling to find your voice online? What if your authentic expertise was all you needed to create powerful content?" (Problem/Promise)
*   **Proof:** "MarketingOS transforms your natural conversations into a complete content engine. See how founders like [Founder Name] are building influence and growing their businesses, simply by being themselves." (Demonstration/Social Proof)
*   **Call to Action:** "Unlock your authentic content. Start your MarketingOS journey today." (Direct CTA)

### 7. Creative asset backlog
1.  **Core Landing Page Copy & Wireframe:** (High Priority)
2.  **Brand Style Guide (Visual & Tone):** (High Priority)
3.  **Short Explainer Video Script & Storyboard:** (Medium Priority)
4.  **3-5 Founder Testimonial Quotes/Graphics:** (Medium Priority)
5.  **Email Welcome Sequence Copy:** (Medium Priority)
6.  **Social Media Launch Kit (5-7 posts):** (Low Priority)

### 8. Confidence and biggest unknown
**Confidence:** High. The core promise of authentic, founder-led content is clear and directly addresses stated pain points.
**Biggest Unknown:** The specific 'feel' of the AI coaching interaction. How conversational and natural can it truly be, and how will that translate into the user's perception of 'authenticity'? This will heavily influence the tone of all creative assets.
